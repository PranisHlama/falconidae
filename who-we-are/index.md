# Who we are

Falconidae Export and Import Hub is a Nepal-based import and export company with a strong role in distribution and market connection. We help international producers and exporters introduce suitable products to Nepali customers, while helping Nepali producers and exporters identify and approach foreign buyers.

We support importers, exporters, suppliers, buyers, and growing businesses that need a practical partner to help manage cross-border requirements. We listen first, clarify the scope, and coordinate the right people and documents for the job.

Our work is grounded in Nepal while connected to international supply and customer networks. Our Nepali customer portfolio is an important advantage for foreign sellers and buyers evaluating the Nepali market. We also research specific markets, customer needs, product opportunities, and marketing approaches before recommending a commercial path.

## Our role

We help turn a trade requirement into an organized process. Depending on the assignment, that may include sourcing, supplier communication, quotation coordination, document preparation, freight coordination, shipment tracking, and delivery follow-up.

Our exact role is shaped around the client’s needs. We can support a single shipment, a recurring supply arrangement, or early-stage exploration of a new market.

## What makes us different

- A practical distribution position in the Nepali market.
- Customer and business connections that help foreign applicants assess local opportunity.
- Research for Nepali and foreign producers, exporters, sellers, and buyers.
- Market-specific research and marketing support.
- A flexible approach covering consulting, sourcing, sales, import, and export.

## Our approach

We value honesty over overpromising, consistency over shortcuts, and relationships over transactions. When a requirement needs clarification or a route needs adjustment, we communicate early and work toward a sensible solution.

## What clients can expect

- A clear understanding of the requirement before outreach begins.
- A practical explanation of what Falconidae can coordinate directly and what belongs with an appointed specialist.
- Organized communication between clients, suppliers, buyers, forwarders, and other stakeholders.
- Timely notice when specifications, documents, availability, cost, or timing changes.
- A written record of the important commercial and shipment details.

## How we work with partners

We aim to build relationships based on accurate information, respectful communication, and realistic commitments. We assess opportunities according to the product, parties involved, destination, timing, and available documentation rather than treating every inquiry as automatically feasible.

## Our promise

We will listen carefully, communicate plainly, protect the confidentiality of information entrusted to us, and take responsibility for the coordination within our agreed scope.
