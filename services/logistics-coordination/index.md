# Logistics coordination

We coordinate with relevant logistics and forwarding partners to help keep a shipment’s movement visible and organized from dispatch through delivery.

Nepal shipments may involve multimodal movement, border transfers, transshipment, or an airport connection. We help stakeholders understand the planned handoffs and surface changes early; the appointed carrier or forwarder confirms the operational route, booking, rates, and transport conditions.

## Coordination may include

- Route and mode discussion
- Freight and forwarding communication
- Pickup and dispatch coordination
- Shipment-status updates
- Delivery planning and follow-up
- Issue escalation between stakeholders

Final routes, costs, transit times, and carrier arrangements depend on the shipment and are confirmed before proceeding.

## What coordination looks like

We help establish the shipment information, request or compare logistics details, confirm the handoff points, and keep the relevant parties aligned. During movement, we pass along status updates and help surface exceptions such as missing documents, delayed pickup, transshipment changes, or delivery issues.

## Before dispatch

The parties should confirm cargo description, quantity, dimensions and weight, packaging, pickup and delivery addresses, delivery terms, insurance expectations, required documents, access restrictions, and the contact responsible at each handoff.

Falconidae does not become the carrier, insurer, customs broker, or warehouse operator unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise. Third-party terms and limitations continue to apply.
