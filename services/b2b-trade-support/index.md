# B2B trade and market support

We help businesses evaluate opportunities, communicate with prospective partners, and create a practical path from introduction to transaction.

Our research can support foreign companies assessing Nepal, Nepali producers seeking overseas buyers, and sellers or buyers looking for a suitable commercial partner. We study the specific market, product category, customer profile, competition, and practical route before coordinating outreach where appropriate.

## Support may include

- Supplier and buyer introductions
- Product and market inquiry coordination
- Commercial communication support
- Requirement matching
- Recurring-order coordination
- Relationship and follow-up management
- Nepal distribution and customer-access support
- Market and marketing research

We believe a useful introduction is only the beginning. The goal is to create clear expectations and a workable relationship for both sides.

## How we add structure

We can help prepare an inquiry brief, organize product and company information, coordinate introductions, record questions and answers, and keep follow-up moving after the first conversation. Where there is mutual interest, we help the parties clarify volume, specifications, timing, pricing basis, payment expectations, delivery terms, and next actions.

## Responsible introductions

Introductions are based on the information available and are not a guarantee of a party’s performance, solvency, product quality, licensing, or willingness to transact. Each party should complete its own commercial, legal, compliance, and credit checks before committing.

For recurring trade, we can help document a repeatable communication and order process so expectations remain consistent as volumes or counterparties change.

## Fees and agreement

The applicant is responsible for the agreed commission. Falconidae charges commission for consultancy services and, where agreed, from completed or facilitated sales. The applicable rate is determined by written agreement. A contract between the applicant and Falconidae must be concluded before the engagement begins.
