# Import and sourcing

We help businesses explore and manage import requirements by bringing structure to sourcing, supplier communication, specifications, quotations, and order coordination.

For Nepal-bound imports, early planning can help identify the likely border or airport route, transit arrangements, duties and taxes to be confirmed, product permissions, labeling expectations, and the documents required by the relevant authorities and service providers. We coordinate the inquiry; licensed professionals remain responsible for formal customs and regulatory advice.

## Support may include

- Requirement and specification review
- Supplier and product inquiry coordination
- Quotation and lead-time comparison
- Sample and quality-discussion coordination
- Purchase and shipment planning
- Import documentation coordination

Share your requirement with product details, quantity, preferred origin, destination, and target timeline. We will help define the next step.

## A typical sourcing workflow

We first clarify the requirement and separate essential specifications from preferences. We then identify or contact suitable supply options, collect comparable information, and discuss price, minimum order, lead time, packaging, and payment expectations. Where appropriate, we coordinate samples or additional quality information before the parties decide whether to proceed.

## Information that improves results

Include the intended use, technical or quality standards, acceptable alternatives, target quantity, order frequency, packaging, required certificates, destination, and preferred commercial terms. Clear requirements reduce unsuitable quotations and avoid rework later.

Supplier identification and product availability are subject to verification, response, compliance, and commercial agreement. We do not treat an introduction or quotation as an endorsement or guarantee.
