# Our services

From a first sourcing inquiry to final delivery follow-up, Falconidae offers practical support across the trade process.

Our Nepal-based team can coordinate with clients, suppliers, buyers, freight forwarders, customs stakeholders, inspection providers, warehouses, and other relevant partners. The exact responsibilities, fees, and handoffs are agreed in writing for each assignment.

## Commercial engagement

For consulting, market research, introductions, distribution, or sales support, concluding a written contract between the applicant and Falconidae is obligatory. Consultancy and sales services are commission-based. The commission rate, payment timing, scope, and responsibilities are determined according to the agreement before work begins.

## Choose the level of support you need

Some clients need help with one defined task, such as a supplier inquiry or document checklist. Others need coordination across sourcing, order preparation, freight, and delivery follow-up. We can discuss a focused assignment or a connected workflow for recurring requirements.

## Across every service

We emphasize a clear brief, documented assumptions, responsive communication, and appropriate handoffs. The final scope depends on the product, route, counterparties, applicable requirements, and providers appointed for the transaction.

## Start with a conversation

Send us the product or service requirement, expected volume, origin and destination, timing, and any known standards or documents. We will help determine which service areas are relevant.
