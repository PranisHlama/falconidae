# Export support

We support suppliers and businesses preparing to reach buyers in new markets. Our work is centered on clear product information, professional coordination, and shipment readiness.

For exporters in Nepal, this may include organizing product information for international buyers, coordinating samples and quotations, preparing a shipment brief, and aligning packaging, pickup, transport, and document expectations. Export eligibility, certificates, inspections, and government approvals remain product- and destination-specific.

## Support may include

- Buyer inquiry and communication support
- Product information and quotation coordination
- Order and quantity confirmation
- Packaging and dispatch requirement coordination
- Export-document information gathering
- Shipment and delivery follow-up

Market access, product eligibility, and regulatory requirements vary by product and destination. We confirm the applicable scope for each assignment.

## Preparing for a buyer conversation

A professional export inquiry usually needs a consistent product description, specifications, photographs or technical information where relevant, available quantity, minimum order, packaging, production lead time, price basis, payment expectations, and destination. We help organize this information so prospective buyers can evaluate the offer efficiently.

## From interest to dispatch

Once a buyer’s requirement is understood, we can help coordinate clarification, quotation, order confirmation, packaging expectations, document information, pickup arrangements, and shipment updates. The exporter remains responsible for the accuracy of product claims and for meeting applicable legal and quality obligations.

We may recommend specialist advice when a product requires permits, testing, certification, labeling review, or destination-specific approval.
