# Frequently asked questions

## Where is Falconidae based?

Falconidae Import and Export Pvt. Ltd. is based in Nepal and supports domestic businesses, overseas partners, importers, exporters, and international trade inquiries.

Our business operates as Falconidae Export and Import Hub, with import, export, distribution, consulting, market research, and marketing support as core activities.

## What products do you import or export?

Our product scope depends on the requirement, destination, applicable regulations, and our ability to verify a suitable supply or buyer. Contact us with the product details for an initial discussion.

We may explore products such as agricultural and natural products, handicrafts, textiles, machinery, packaging, consumer goods, and industrial goods when the product, parties, route, and approvals are suitable. We do not handle prohibited or unlawful trade.

Our import categories include furniture, kitchen products, marble, textiles, cosmetics, clothing, fashion wear, vehicle parts, electronics, computers and accessories, musical instruments, stationery, dairy products, and chocolates. Our export categories include handicrafts, Thanka and paintings, wool, carpets, statues and idols, Pashmina, Khukuri, honey, and dairy products.

## Do you handle one-time shipments?

Yes. We can discuss one-time, recurring, and project-based requirements, subject to scope and feasibility.

## Can you help find suppliers or buyers?

We can coordinate sourcing inquiries, introductions, and communication where there is a suitable and verifiable opportunity.

## Do you provide customs clearance?

We can coordinate with relevant logistics and customs stakeholders. The exact service and responsibility depend on the shipment and the appointed licensed providers.

## How do I begin?

Send the product, quantity, origin, destination, and timeline. We will review the information and let you know what details are needed next.

## Can you guarantee a price or delivery date?

No. Pricing, availability, routing, transit time, duties, and delivery dates depend on the product, parties, service providers, market conditions, and approvals. We share confirmed information and identify assumptions clearly.

## Can you arrange customs clearance in Nepal?

We can coordinate with the appointed customs broker, freight forwarder, and other relevant stakeholders. Customs declarations, classification, valuation, duties, taxes, permits, and formal representation must be handled by the responsible licensed party and confirmed for the specific shipment.

## Can you work with our existing supplier or forwarder?

Yes. We can coordinate with your existing partners where the responsibilities and communication process are clear. We can also discuss suitable providers for a new requirement.

## Do you inspect or certify products?

We can coordinate requests for samples, specifications, inspections, or certificates with the relevant parties. Actual testing, certification, inspection, and regulatory approval must be performed by the authorized or qualified organization.

## What information should we prepare?

Prepare the product description, specifications, quantity, packaging, origin, destination, desired Incoterm if known, target timing, and any required permits or standards. We can help identify missing information.

## Do you serve individuals?

Our primary focus is business-to-business trade and commercial requirements. We review individual inquiries case by case based on product, volume, destination, and feasibility.

## How are fees and responsibilities agreed?

The scope, coordination fee or commission, third-party charges, payment terms, and responsibilities are confirmed in writing before work begins. Government charges, freight, insurance, inspection, storage, and other external costs may be separate.

## Is a contract required?

Yes. Applicants must conclude a contract with Falconidae before consultancy, market research, distribution, introduction, or sales-support work begins. The agreement sets out the scope, commission rate, payment terms, responsibilities, and applicable conditions.
