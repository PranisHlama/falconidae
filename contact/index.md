# Let’s talk about your trade requirement

Whether you are sourcing a product, preparing an export, looking for a supply partner, or exploring a new market, send us the details and we will respond with a practical next step.

Falconidae is based in Nepal and welcomes inquiries from Nepali businesses, overseas buyers and suppliers, logistics partners, and organizations exploring lawful cross-border trade connected to Nepal.

## Include, if available

- Product or service name
- Quantity or expected order volume
- Origin and destination
- Required specifications or quality standards
- Preferred timeline
- Any documentation or logistics needs

## Inquiry form

Suggested fields: Name, company, email, phone, country, inquiry type, product or requirement, quantity, destination, timeline, and message.

By submitting an inquiry, you agree that Falconidae may use the information to respond to your request. See our [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy/index.md).

## What happens after you contact us

We review the information provided, identify any essential gaps, and respond with questions or a suggested next step. A response may involve a discovery call, supplier or buyer outreach, document review, or a recommendation to involve a licensed specialist.

An inquiry does not create an agency relationship, reserve inventory, confirm a quotation, or guarantee that a transaction can proceed. Commercial terms and responsibilities are agreed separately in writing.

## Sensitive information

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, confidential personal records, or documents that are not necessary for an initial assessment. If detailed records are required, we will indicate a suitable way to share them.
