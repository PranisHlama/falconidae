# Connecting quality products with the right markets

Falconidae Export and Import Hub is a well-established Nepal-based import, export, distribution, sourcing, and trade-consulting company. We connect Nepali customers with reliable international products while helping Nepali producers and exporters reach suitable foreign markets.

## Trade made clear

International trade involves many moving parts. Our role is to make those parts easier to manage—from understanding your requirement and identifying suitable supply options to coordinating documentation and shipment updates.

Whether you are looking to import products reliably or introduce products to new markets, we approach every assignment with care, transparency, and attention to detail.

## Nepal as a trade partner

From our base in Nepal, we serve as a distribution partner in the Nepali market and support businesses navigating cross-border purchasing, export preparation, freight coordination, and documentation. Nepal’s land-linked trade routes, border handoffs, transit requirements, and product-specific approvals make early planning especially important.

## What we can help with

- Import coordination and product sourcing
- Export support and market-ready shipment preparation
- Supplier and buyer communication
- Documentation and trade-compliance coordination
- Freight, forwarding, and delivery coordination
- Customized solutions for recurring or project-based requirements
- Distribution and market-entry support in Nepal

## Areas we can explore

Our import portfolio includes furniture, kitchen products, marble, textiles, cosmetics, clothing and fashion wear, vehicle parts, electronics, computers and accessories, musical instruments, stationery, dairy products, chocolates, and other suitable product categories.

Our export portfolio includes handicrafts, Thanka and paintings, wool, carpets, statues and idols, Pashmina, Khukuri, honey, dairy products, and other Nepali products with suitable quality, documentation, and market potential. Product acceptance is always subject to specifications, market demand, permits, quality requirements, sanctions, and a transaction-specific feasibility review.

## A straightforward way to get started

1. **Share the requirement.** Tell us what you need, where it should come from or go to, the expected volume, and when you need it.
2. **Clarify the route.** We review specifications, commercial expectations, documentation, and practical constraints with you.
3. **Coordinate the next action.** Depending on the opportunity, that may be a supplier inquiry, buyer introduction, quotation review, document checklist, or logistics discussion.

## Who we support
