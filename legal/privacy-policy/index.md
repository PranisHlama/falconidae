We may use information to respond to inquiries, evaluate whether we can support a requirement, coordinate requested introductions or services, maintain business records, improve our communications, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, ongoing business records, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.
