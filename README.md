# Falconidae Import and Export Pvt. Ltd. — Website Content

This folder contains the website’s page content in Markdown. Each page uses YAML front matter followed by the copy intended for the rendered site.

## Content map

- `home.md` — homepage messaging and primary calls to action
- `who-we-are.md` — company introduction and positioning
- `what-we-do.md` — capabilities and operating process
- `about-us.md` — values, standards, and long-term direction
- `services/` — detailed import, export, sourcing, logistics, and compliance services
- `contact.md` — contact page and inquiry form copy
- `faq.md` — common customer questions
- `legal/` — privacy and terms starter copy

## Publishing notes

Replace all bracketed placeholders before launch. Product categories, countries served, certifications, delivery timelines, and claims about experience should be confirmed by the Falconidae team before publication.

## Content principles

The copy is written to present Falconidae as a practical coordination partner rather than a replacement for licensed customs brokers, freight forwarders, insurers, lawyers, tax advisers, or regulators. Keep that distinction clear whenever a page is edited.

Use specific, verifiable information wherever possible. Avoid promising fixed prices, delivery dates, product availability, regulatory outcomes, or market access unless those details have been confirmed for a particular transaction.

## Recommended launch checklist

- Confirm the legal company name, registration details, office address, and operating locations.
- Replace the email, phone, WhatsApp, privacy contact, and business-hours placeholders.
- Confirm the product categories, origins, destinations, and service partners Falconidae can support.
- Add approved leadership, experience, certifications, memberships, and customer proof points only when documented.
- Have the privacy policy and terms reviewed for the company’s jurisdiction and actual data practices.
- Test every internal link, inquiry-form field, consent notice, and mobile layout.
